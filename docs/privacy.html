Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Vantum ("we", "us") operates the @vantum.tech and @vantum.bio accounts and publishes its own news content to them automatically through the YouTube Data API (YouTube API Services), the TikTok Content Posting API and the Instagram Graph API.

What we collect

This app does not collect, store, or process personal data belonging to users, viewers, or the general public on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram or anywhere else. It has no public-facing website, login flow, or sign-up form for third parties, and it is not offered to anyone outside Vantum.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services to upload Vantum's own videos to the YouTube channels of @vantum.tech and @vantum.bio, which we own and operate. By using this app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

The app signs in to our own Google account with OAuth solely to upload videos to our own channels. It does not read, collect, store, or share any YouTube user data, including other channels' data, comments or viewer information. The only data it keeps is the OAuth token for our own account, stored encrypted as a repository secret and used only to upload scheduled posts. Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page.

TikTok account access

This app uses TikTok's Login Kit solely to authenticate the @vantum.tech and @vantum.bio accounts, which are owned and operated by us, in order to post content to those accounts via the Content Posting API. No other TikTok accounts are accessed, and no data belonging to other TikTok users is read, stored, or shared.

Instagram account access

This app uses the Instagram Graph API solely to publish posts to the @vantum.tech and @vantum.bio Instagram accounts, which we own and operate. No data belonging to other Instagram users is read, stored, or shared.

Content sources

Content posted by this app is built from publicly available news reporting and openly licensed (CC0, public domain, or CC BY) photography and footage, and company press-kit images cleared for editorial use, each credited in the post. No private or personal data is used in generating this content.

Data retention

Access tokens issued by Google, TikTok and Meta for the @vantum.tech and @vantum.bio accounts are stored securely and used only to publish scheduled posts. No other data is retained.

Data deletion

Because this app stores no data about YouTube, TikTok or Instagram users or viewers, there is no user data to delete. The only stored credentials are the access tokens for our own accounts; they are deleted from our systems when access is revoked (for Google, on the Google security settings page) or when the app is retired.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Vantum by direct message to @vantum.tech on Instagram.
