Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Vantum ("we", "us") operates the @vantum.tech and @vantum.bio accounts and uses the TikTok Content Posting API to publish content automatically.

What we collect

This app does not collect, store, or process personal data belonging to TikTok users, viewers, or the general public. It does not have a public-facing website, login flow, or sign-up form for third parties.

TikTok account access

This app uses TikTok's Login Kit solely to authenticate the @vantum.tech and @vantum.bio accounts, which are owned and operated by us, in order to post content to those accounts via the Content Posting API. No other TikTok accounts are accessed, and no data belonging to other TikTok users is read, stored, or shared.

Content sources

Content posted by this app is built from publicly available news reporting and openly licensed (CC0, public domain, or CC BY) photography and video, each credited in the post. No private or personal data is used in generating this content.

Data retention

Access tokens issued by TikTok for the @vantum.tech and @vantum.bio accounts are stored securely and used only to publish scheduled posts. No other data is retained.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the account holder via the contact information on file with TikTok for Developers.
